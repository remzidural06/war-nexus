War Nexus — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 10, 2026

Gaming Stars FZ-LLC ("we", "our", "us") operates the War Nexus mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you sign in using Google, Email, or as a Guest, we collect your email address, display name, and a unique user identifier.

Game Data: We store your game progress including base buildings, units, resources, research, battle reports, and alliance membership on our servers (Firebase).

Chat Messages: Messages sent in global chat, alliance chat, and direct messages are stored on our servers.

Device Information: We collect Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens to send push notifications. We do not collect device identifiers, location data, or contacts.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the game experience
  • To save and sync your game progress across devices
  • To enable multiplayer features (alliances, PvP, chat)
  • To send push notifications about attacks and game events
  • To display leaderboards and player rankings
  • To prevent cheating and enforce fair play

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers (Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Functions) with industry-standard encryption. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties.

4. In-App Purchases

War Nexus offers optional in-app purchases for virtual currency (Gold). All payments are processed by Google Play or Apple App Store. We do not collect or store payment information.

5. Children's Privacy

War Nexus is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

6. Push Notifications

We send push notifications for game events (attacks, battle results, alliance wars). You can disable specific notification categories in the app's Profile settings, or disable all notifications through your device settings.

7. Chat & User-Generated Content

Chat messages are filtered for profanity and spam. We reserve the right to moderate and remove inappropriate content. Users can be banned for violating community guidelines.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data by contacting us at remzidural06@gmail.com. Upon request, we will delete your account data within 30 days.

9. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — Authentication, database, cloud functions, messaging
  • Google Play Services — Sign-in, in-app purchases

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app or on this page.
